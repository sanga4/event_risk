# Phase I — Chronological historical-market placebo and factor risk diagnostics

Research script: `lab/phase1_historical_placebo_holdout.py`. Its inputs are the existing 11.1-million-row historical market-snapshot **derived venue/day features** and 18 Treasury factor source series; it does not reload all raw market snapshots or modify original source, model or v4 event records.

The existing retrospective historical aggregation spans 2021–2026 and provides 729 qualifying Kalshi venue-days and 937 Polymarket venue-days with >=10 comparable market quotes. Factor data span 2024–2026 and are *historically revised/backfilled*, not original market-participant first-known-at values. The previous 36 same-day univariate OLS research associations fit approximately 492 Kalshi and 640 Polymarket matched sessions without chronological holdout. Their in-sample R² was at most ~0.00752 and must not be treated as causal event impact.

**This independent validation uses a common complete calendar-date sample for each venue/factor and for both test models.** Factors are deduplicated by factor ID/date and latest ingestion; bps changes are calculated from consecutive observed yield levels if the prior observation is within seven calendar days. The predictor is the mean absolute daily movement in percentage points of event-market contract probability at the venue level, with >=10 comparable markets. Its measured date is historical event timestamp, not a verified original quote-capture/publication time.

For every factor × venue, split the paired observed dates **chronologically**: 70% training, 30% untouched heldout. Only training rows estimate the regression slope/intercept and factor's mean. Evaluate test squared error relative to the fixed training-factor mean baseline. This defines a test-R²-like skill score that can be negative. The **negative control** replaces same-day market volatility with **the NEXT observed venue-day market volatility** as a deliberately unavailable, future-dated placebo, fitted *only on the same training rows*. Both use identical paired rows, holdout dates and baseline. The future feature is forbidden in live serving or backtesting: any apparent predictive strength is evidence that time confounding, common regimes or data leakage need review, not a trading forecast.

Acceptance enforces 18 factor families and two venues (36 research comparisons), minimum 450 matched days, 18 months, >=300 training / >=120 heldout dates, strict training-last before test-first, stable unique date partitions, finite factor and model moments, zero false upstream PIT/decision-use approvals and source-manifest lineage. The 450/18-month limits are reproducible **research screens**, not sufficient institutional validation standards. The same-day and next-day coefficients are not causal estimands; do not use p-values from naïve repeated quote rows.

Append-only outputs are `lab_historical_placebo_holdout_builds_v1` and `lab_historical_placebo_holdout_models_v1`, with source-code SHA and input historic build identity, fixed train/test dates and counts, heldout baseline skill, placebo skill and explicit FALSE PIT / causal mapping / institutional risk flags. The exact 36 cases are preserved; no model selection after the holdout, no optimization on future placebo, no backtest certification, and no independent original source publication clocks.

**Remaining P0 production requirements:** external original provider publication vintages and revision histories; rights for market quote histories and factor levels; issuer corporate action / security identifiers; economically prespecified event-channel mapping and surprise instruments; independent model-risk committee review, confounder sensitivity, longer true prospective calendar date coverage and suitable clustered/serial-dependence-aware inference.
