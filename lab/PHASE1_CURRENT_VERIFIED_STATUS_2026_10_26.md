## Model evidence interpretation

The source-locked full 48-family 10-date-block paired bootstrap has one exploratory full-training heldout loss-improvement pass, **SBUX**, but zero passes for the alternative training-tail-exclusion variant. **No ticker passes both** predeclared model specifications. Aggregate cross-security mean daily squared-error improvement is **−29.46 bp²** (95% block CI **[−333.13,+270.59]**) full-training; **−167.67 bp²** (95% block CI **[−563.39,+173.56]**) tail-excluded. These intervals include zero. SBUX does not have a robust contemporaneous funding coefficient: its full vs trimmed funding beta **reverses sign** and neither reaches a significant global Holm p-value.

A separate **exploratory post-hoc** first-vs-second chronological half of the 209 actual 48-security common heldout dates showed a striking regime reversal in mean daily loss difference: full training **+316.74 bp²** first 104 dates versus **−372.37 bp²** last 105; tail-excluded **+229.78** first and **−561.34 bp²** last. The ten greatest absolute across-security daily differences contribute **34.9%** full and **34.5%** tail-excluded of total absolute daily differences. Do **not** turn a favorable earlier sample or post-hoc outlier exclusions into a newly predeclared significance claim. These effects require independent temporal/regime validation on later original-source data. Aggregate full-horizon nonpositive mean forecast value and empty two-variant Holm candidate intersection already block promotion.

## Critical path to institutional Phase I

1. **Acquire verifiable as-first-known source evidence**: Treasury and NY Fed rate provider publication and revision clocks, Kalshi/Polymarket original venue market/quote publication/version clocks, economic first release and pre-event survey consensus vintages, and source contractual use/redistribution permissions. Keep warehouse HTTP receipt clocks separate.
2. **Source-backfill missing institutional data contracts**: permanent issuer IDs, delisted/security survivorship universe, original split/dividend announcement clocks and total-return adjustments, calendars of economic surprises with native units, licensed credit/futures/options sources and reviewed security/factor link evidence. The 135 missing fields/255 uncertified source rules cannot be cleared by arbitrary Null/backfilled row creation.
3. **Collect more genuinely prospective events and rate innovations** over at least 60 independent capture calendar days, 60 source runs, 30 observed rate-factor dates and multiple distinct factor source values per series. More tickers and repeated 18-factor labels are not independent macro observations.
4. **Rebuild and independently validate the actual event→economic transmission→financial factor beta models**, with true first-release surprises, arrival timing, overlapping macro events, calendar cluster dependence, source no-lookahead, negative controls, structural-break/regime holdouts, robust cross-asset covariance shrinkage/portfolio losses and reviewer-proven mapping decisions.
5. **Institutional release**: least-privilege database/API, proven data-rights approval, authenticated research endpoints, operational freshness monitors, alerting/rollback, monitoring and completed manual governance. Keep production release FALSE until a new evaluated gate manifest plus independent human signoffs can legitimately pass.
