    What is connected

    The website reads its source-published 543 securities, 108 factor definitions, and 22 illustrative economic channels from Preview MVP's own catalog API. Historical funding-regression outputs and market questions are permissioned research data, not unrestricted public content.

    Event Attribution

    Type ticker symbols and position dollar amounts directly into the browser. The app calculates an analyst-prior relevance score for each holding and a portfolio-weighted relevance total, using the repository's original entity-to-security seed relationships. Those 0–1 weights are not risk betas. If you enter your own hypothetical return changes, a separate what-if P&L calculation uses only those explicit assumptions. No holdings are uploaded or sent to Preview MVP APIs.

    Data quality boundaries

    Scenario-to-channel and channel-to-factor links are hypotheses, not causal estimates. An observed historical factor correlation is not the effect of the event. The currently deployed source graph does not publish live market odds or first-published-vintage surprise shocks; permissioned real questions remain locked until source redistribution and user access controls are approved.

    A zero mapped relevance score means no matching seed relationship, not zero economic exposure. Gross dollar weights avoid canceling long and short positions in the relevance display. For holdings with unknown mappings we report coverage separately, never assign a zero measured beta.
