MODEL BOUNDARIES / METHODOLOGY

What the EventRisk graph represents

A research interface joining five qualitative node types. It is an architectural preview, not a model of the price change caused by an event.

01

Events and reported releases

Most event nodes are openly labeled hypothetical scenarios. The two BEA nodes contain genuinely reported August 2026 macro actuals from public official pages retrieved later in October. They are not first-release surprises or live events.

02

Entities and securities

Entity-to-security links are derived from the repository's public seeds/exposures.csv and securities.csv. Weights are hand-written or rule-generated analyst-prior relevance weights, not statistically estimated betas or portfolio exposure coefficients.

03

Economic transmission

Event-to-channel and channel-to-factor relations are illustrative hypotheses, not certified mappings or signs of causality. The financial factor names and definitions come from the repository's qualitative seeds/factors.json catalogue.

04

Statistical and production checks

The last executed institutional release-gate snapshot passed 25/54 automatic checks and left four manual signoffs; full institutional release remains blocked. The lab reports 96 funding model fits with zero global Holm-surviving coefficients. This graph does not change those facts.
